Before sending this home, check by eye:
  * every line above says PASS. 'arm=X confirmed' means the NoC each
    RISC actually used was read back out of the trace and matched the
    arm -- a run without it measured something other than what it says.
  * ARM A IS THE CONTROL, and the indented line under each run carries
    that run's observed per-class latencies. There is NO prior card
    session on wormhole, so arm A ESTABLISHES the control
    rather than reproducing one, and the 375-382 / 480-510 band quoted
    in the README is BLACKHOLE's -- do not read this part against it.
    What arm A is checked against is the simulator side run at the same
    --arch, --bytes and --chunks, at home. What you CAN check here is
    that the repeats agree and that the two size arms differ.
  * the 3 repeats of an arm agree. They are separate runs of an
    identical program, so a big spread means something else was on the
    card and the session is suspect. Do NOT re-run and keep the better
    session -- say so in the handover instead.
  * the size arms DISAGREE with each other. They differ only in transfer
    size and are supposed to separate the per-hop term from the per-byte
    one; identical numbers mean one arm did not run the way it was meant to.
